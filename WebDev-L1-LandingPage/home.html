Our Services

"Delivery" Service from Turkey

Kango.az carries out the delivery of goods and postal packages ordered online from Turkish shopping sites. Packages accepted in Turkey are delivered to customers within 3-6 business days. Note that standard products are shipped every business day, while liquid and battery products are shipped 1 or 2 times a week.

Direct Delivery from Trendyol

You can order directly from Trendyol.com to your Azerbaijan address by selecting over 35 Kango "PICK UP" points.

Returns (From Turkey)

We process returns to the seller store for defective, incorrect, or damaged items received at our Turkey warehouse, as well as any package you wish to return (the seller store's return service must be active for this).

"Courier" Service

We also organize the delivery of your packages that have arrived at the Baku warehouse to any address. This saves you time. You can get information about prices in the rates section.

Top-up Balance via Emanat

You can top up your Kango.az account balance via E-manat payment terminals.
