ABOUT US

LyakGo operates as a freight delivery company. We set out to become Azerbaijan's fastest and most reliable courier company. Our company was established to make Turkish online shopping pages more accessible. Our operating principle is to deliver products to our customers more conveniently and at more affordable prices that all Turkish online shopping stores (Trendyol, Morhipo, Hepsiburada, Koton, Zara, LC Waikiki, etc.) refuse to ship directly to Azerbaijan. Our company carries out deliveries both domestically and from Turkey under the license obtained in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Azerbaijan. We strive to be closer to our customers with 35 branches. Our goal is to serve our state and nation in all regions of Azerbaijan. Based on our prior experience, we assure you that we will provide high-quality service.
